- **The reviewer disproved two of the builder's git claims by experiment.** (1) The "union of one or more reproduced blocks" model is half of git: `xdl_merge` at `XDL_MERGE_ZEALOUS` both joins conflicts ≤3 lines apart **and splits** ones whose sides share lines, while `--diff3` caps at `EAGER` and does neither — so a pristine file can be 2 blocks on disk and 1 reproduced, and `attach_base` refuses it saying "base unavailable after edits" to a user who edited nothing. (2) Reproduced bytes are not disk bytes under `eol=crlf`/`autocrlf`: stages are LF, the working tree is CRLF, so **every** block mismatches. Held: the `=======` rule, the 3/4-line gap, CRLF round-trip, no-final-newline, the blob id, all seven XY pairs.

The gap was recorded honestly by the builder, but the choice it recorded was the unsafe one of the two the ticket offered. **broken**, not unverified.
