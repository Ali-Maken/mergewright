## Major

### Major 1 — `Tab` in the branch picker at a live stop hands the keyboard to the banner; the letters he then types run `c` / `s` / `a` behind the open picker
- **Where:** `crates/graft/src/update/keys.rs:150` (`banner_key`, whose `take_focus` claims `Tab`) runs before
  `:166` (`picker::picker_key`). `crates/graft/src/update/banner.rs:333-352` `take_focus` checks `banner_focus`,
  `banner_view` and the detail pane — not the picker. `picker::opens` (`update/picker.rs:120-129`) allows `C`
  whenever the graph has focus, which during a live operation is every `edit` stop (07b) and any stop after `Esc`.
- **Observed** (a throwaway test in my copy, through `update::update`): rebase live, graph focused, `C`, list lands,
  `Tab` → `banner_focus = true`, picker still `Some` and `shown`, no `Tab` action of the picker's. Then:
  shell-begun rebase, `c` → `Command::ContinueInGit { kind: Rebase }` (the terminal goes to `git rebase --continue`);
  Graft-begun rebase, `s` → the skip confirm box opens. The picker box and its bar (`HintContext::Picker`,
  `view/rows.rs:719-721`) stay on screen the whole time, saying he is typing a branch name.
- **Why it matters:** a visible text field that lets letters through to an operation's keys (keymap §2.1: a text
  field claims every key but `Ctrl+C`). "cool-feature" typed after a `Tab` continues the rebase. The picker's own
  `Tab` ("show it on the graph", ticket 10 Decided) never happens at a stop.
- **Fix:** the picker is a text field; claim before the banner. Either route `picker::picker_key` for a *shown*
  picker ahead of `banner_key` in `key_event` (the open-on-`C` half can stay where it is), or have `take_focus` return
  `false` while `modal_owns_keys()`. Red test first: the probe above, asserting `Tab` closes the picker with the cursor
  on the tip and `banner_focus` untouched, and that `c`/`s`/`a` are typed.

### Major 2 — the still-to-apply block is drawn but not counted: clicks land on the wrong commit and `graph_rows()` over-counts
- **Where:** 07c adds `ScreenRows::pending` between the banner and the rows (`graft-ui/src/graph_screen/layout.rs`,
  `screen_rows`, the `pending` rect: up to half the free rows). The app's two mirrors of that layout do not know it:
  `crates/graft/src/update/geometry.rs:23-27` `rows_above_graph` (header + harness banner + tab rows) and
  `crates/graft/src/update/state.rs:473-489` `graph_rows` (no term for it, though `preview_rows_taken` shows the
  pattern).
- **Observed** (throwaway test in `m7_walk/rewrite_stops.rs`, the 07b `stopped_on_a1` fixture, 120×40): `c1` is drawn on
  screen row 6 (the block takes rows 1–5); a left click on row 6 selects **`m1`**, five rows down; `graph_rows() = 38`.
- **Why it matters:** at an `edit` stop the graph has the keyboard *and* the mouse, and the next keys act on the
  selected row (`a` amends only on HEAD/uncommitted, `.`/right-click open a menu for the row, `o` takes it). A click
  on the stopped commit selects another. `graph_rows` also drives `j`/`k` scrolling and paging, so the cursor can
  move onto rows the block pushed off the bottom (inferred from the count; not walked). `picker_box_in`
  (`update/picker.rs:256`) reads the same `rows_above_graph`.
- **Fix:** one function for "rows the graph body starts below", mirroring `screen_rows`' pending height
  (`still_to_apply_height` capped at half, ≥ 3 or 0), used by `rows_above_graph` and subtracted in `graph_rows`, as
  `preview_rows_taken` is. Red test: click on a drawn row under the block selects that row.
