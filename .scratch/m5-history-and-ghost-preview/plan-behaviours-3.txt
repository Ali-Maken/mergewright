## Part 2 — what each kind of stop leaves behind (review M7)

The review recommended matching a live journal entry by comparing its
recorded before-state against `ORIG_HEAD`. This record is why that was not
built: **a conflicted cherry-pick and a conflicted revert set no `ORIG_HEAD`
at all**, so the comparison would have rejected Graft's own entry for two of
the four kinds. The guard as built uses `rebase-merge/head-name` for a
rebase, and the current symbolic `HEAD` plus the branch still standing at the
entry's recorded tip for merge, cherry-pick and revert. A rebase with no
`head-name` (a `git am`) matches nothing.
