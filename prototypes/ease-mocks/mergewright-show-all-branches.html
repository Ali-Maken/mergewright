Pick how the graph looks, from inside the app

Merged branches fold by default. To get today's full graph back, pick it as a mode in the ? list. No new key, no file to edit. The app remembers your pick.


  


  

    
A mode, not a key
You pick it once and keep it. It sits with the other things in the ? list, and typing "graph", "fold" or "branch" finds it.

    
You can always tell which mode is on
The top line says 2 merged branches folded or every branch shown. In the list, the mode that's on has the filled dot.

    
Every branch is today's picture
Same rows, same lanes as now. space has nothing to open in that mode, so the bar stops offering it.

  


  
The graph rows are real captures of the app on one test repo, from before the fold work (e31a2d2e) and after it. The ? list and the top-line words are drawn here; the mode isn't built yet (ticket 06h). Captures carry no colour, and the new colours come later this milestone. The app keeps your pick in its settings file ([graph] merged_branches), which you never have to open.
