Merge, and when it clashes, land right on the clash

You're on wallet-release and want integration's latest work in it. Click the terminal and press m. Every key you need is in the bar at the bottom, and every screen tells you what happens next.

What makes it easy

You see it before it happens

m opens a preview: which commits come in, and which files will clash, before anything changes. y goes ahead, as in every other preview in the app.

A stop lands on the first clash

When files clash, the merge stops and you're already in the first one, cursor on the first clash. Last time you had to back out and find them yourself, about 7 keys. Now it's 0.

Only the clash is split

Most of the file is one column, as normal. Only a clashing block splits into yours on the left and theirs on the right, each named with its branch. VS Code went back to this inline style after trying three columns for everything.

Open and solved look different

An open clash has an orange ? and both sides lit. Once you pick, the block shrinks to the result, marked ◀ yours, ▶ theirs or ◆ both, and the side you didn't take is gone. Cyan and pink, not red and green, so colour-blind eyes can tell them apart too.

One key per clash, then it moves on

o takes yours, t theirs, b both, and the cursor jumps to the next open clash in the file, as KDiff3 does. When a file is done it stays on screen, fully solved, so you can read it. n goes to the next file.

The end is a preview, not a surprise

When every clash is solved, y opens the review: “Nothing committed yet”, and what will land, including the files Git combined on its own. y again commits the merge. Esc goes back to the clashes and y brings you back. u undoes a pick at any point.
